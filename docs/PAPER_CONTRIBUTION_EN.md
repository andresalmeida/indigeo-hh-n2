# Candidate contribution statement for the HH manuscript

Prepared text, not a publication or claim of thematic accuracy. Full project-internal evidence and source hashes are retained in the separate 15-row contribution matrix.

## Concise positioning

Aragón-Osejo et al. already provide a 30 m biennial Human Footprint series for mainland Ecuador for 2014–2022. Neither that time span nor 30 m output spacing is therefore claimed as a new contribution of N2. Our contribution is an auditable reconstruction with explicit pressure-source reconciliation, support-aware cantonal summaries, and a reproducible analysis of the information retained by within-canton surface distributions beyond cantonal means.

The supplied ministerial methodology sheet describes national continental reporting; this documents a reporting limitation of that supplied version, not a mathematical impossibility of canton-level calculation. The N2 outputs provide explicit summaries for 218 continental cantons and distinguish annual valid support from the common support across five years. Descriptive bands are a declared adaptation of published intervals, not official MAATE classes or calibrated ecological thresholds.

In the 2020 COMMON-support analysis, Puebloviejo and Manta have robustly close modeled means in the same moderate band but high-pressure surface shares of 2.1741% and 22.0179%, respectively. The 19.8438-percentage-point separation is the largest minimum contrast selected by a stated rule, not a representative case. The full analysis retains all qualifying pairs and support/UTM sensitivities. This result illustrates the complementary information supplied by distributions; it does not establish that a mean is erroneous, locate high-pressure patches or quantify ecological damage.

A separate completed benchmark on 150 complete D0 parcels found similar rank association with a historical visual proxy for SDG15 and N2 (Spearman 0.872 and 0.870). This is not evidence of equivalence, superiority, thematic accuracy or temporal validation. The national reproduction demonstration in this repository regenerates the full cantonal analysis and figure from frozen CSVs; it does not regenerate all HH rasters from primary inputs or establish an entirely Earth Engine implementation. VIIRS version differences, UTM grid-transition limitations and independent temporal validation remain explicit limits. Cross-year results are modeled differences under the recipe, not observed ecological change.

|---|---|---|
| Published SDG15 already includes the 2014–2022 series at 30 m. | Source-by-source reconciliation and an auditable reconstruction with documented substitutions. | Existing temporal coverage/resolution are not presented as novel; agreement with a published map is not ground-truth accuracy. |

| Published maps and historical visual reference provide comparison evidence. | A support-matched, paired rank-association benchmark with explicit exclusions. | Not independent predictions, sample representativeness, equality, thematic accuracy or temporal validation. |
